The basics

What is Hydroponic Farming?

Hydroponic farming is a way of growing plants without traditional soil. The roots get water, nutrients and oxygen directly, in growing conditions you can see and control.

It isn't limited to big commercial greenhouses. The same principles work at very different sizes:

  • Homes
  • Terraces
  • Small farms
  • Commercial spaces
  • Controlled environments
Learn About Hydroponics
Why hydroponics

A practical way to grow with more control

  • Efficient water use

    Many systems recirculate the same nutrient water, so less of it drains away into the ground.

  • Space-friendly growing

    Vertical and compact layouts can fit balconies, terraces and small plots.

  • Controlled conditions

    You manage nutrients, pH and water directly, so you can see and adjust what plants receive.

  • Fresh produce

    Grow close to where you eat or sell, and harvest when you need it.

  • Modern farming

    A measurable way to grow, built on today's tools and knowledge.

  • Scalable systems

    Start with a few channels and add more as your confidence and needs grow.
